# NeXora V5 — pricing notes (Israel, 2026)

V5 positions NeXora as a strong freelancer / small design-development studio using custom Next.js rather than Wix/WordPress templates.

Published Israeli 2026 references used as market anchors include:
- Avinu: business websites from about ₪3,000 up to ₪60,000+ for complex custom development.
- Digitizer: landing pages ₪2,000–7,000; basic business sites ₪5,000–15,000; custom business sites ₪15,000–30,000; e-commerce ₪20,000–50,000.
- Media Deal React/Next.js: professional business website from ₪6,000; e-commerce from ₪8,000; SaaS/complex development from ₪12,000.
- CWS Israel: mid-level freelance developers about ₪1,500–2,400/day in 2026.
- Branding references: basic identity/logo+guidelines commonly starts around ₪3,000 and experienced designers often sit around ₪2,000–5,000 for logo work alone.

NeXora's public starting prices are intentionally below full-service agency custom-build pricing, but above low-cost template/builders:
- Landing: from ₪5,900
- Business website: from ₪11,900
- E-commerce: from ₪16,900
- Digital product / custom web interface: from ₪24,900
- Branding: from ₪4,900

These are starting prices, not fixed quotes. Scope, content, integrations, payment logic, catalog size, backend complexity and languages can materially change the final quote.
